YOUR LOVE WEBSITE
=================

Open index.html in any modern browser.

Quick personalization:
1. Open index.html.
2. Replace "For you" / "Hey, you" with her name or nickname.
3. Edit the three story cards and the four reasons.
4. Replace the letter with your own message.
5. Change the logo initials "P ♥ P" if you want.

No external image files are required. The hero artwork is an original SVG and the rest of the visuals are CSS.
